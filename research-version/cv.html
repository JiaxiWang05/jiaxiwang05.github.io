Research experience

  1. Ongoing

    Student researcher, embodied AI and vision–language–action systems

    Durham University. Supervisors: Dr Amir Atapour-Abarghouei, Dr Chris Chen

    • Cross-embodiment learning, transferable action representations, multimodal robot policies and predictive representations for control.
    • How policies or learned representations generalise across robotic platforms, and how vision–language-conditioned information can be integrated with action and state information.

  2. Jun–Aug 2026

    Summer Undergraduate Research Intern

    Medical Robotics Group, The Chinese University of Hong Kong. Supervisor: Prof. Hongliang Ren. Hong Kong SAR

    • BiliVLA: scene-aware VLA for autonomous ERCP navigation (supervisor Jinsong Lin). Contributed to a system reaching 96.25% mIoU, 91.96% action precision and 84.85% navigation success in ERCP phantom experiments. Supported a two-stage pipeline of grounding-enhanced supervised fine-tuning and GRPO reinforcement optimisation, with scene-aware grounding and safety-aware recovery; integrated hardware, multimodal trajectories and 3-DoF actions.
    • Teleoperated flexible endoscopy benchmark (supervisor Jinsong Lin). Collected 150 synchronised endoscopic video and robot-state segments and resolved timestamp alignment across video, high-frequency robot states and PDT hardware parameters. Built cleaning, frame-extraction, YOLO ROI-cropping and 224 × 224 image–action trajectory workflows; deployed the open-source π0.5 policy as a VLA navigation baseline.
    • Trileg-ESD: vision-guided magnetic soft robot (supervisor Chi Kit Ng). Cooperative VLA algorithm for two soft robots. Structured the motion of a miniature three-legged magnetic soft robot around squatting, leg lifting, rotation, crawling and stance-recovery primitives; investigated a vision–language controller mapping endoscopic frames and motion instructions to voltage commands for three electromagnetic coils.

  3. 2026
