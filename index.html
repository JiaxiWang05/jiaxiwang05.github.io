Now

I study how robots can see, reason and act in places that are hard to model, such as the inside of the body, using vision–language–action and world-action models.

At Durham I work with Dr Amir Atapour-Abarghouei and Dr Chris Chen on how robot policies and learned representations transfer across embodiments. Before that I worked in Prof. Hongliang Ren's Medical Robotics Group at CUHK on autonomous endoscopic navigation.

EndoWAM

Publication Under review

In submissionAAAI 2027 and ICLR 2027arXiv:2608.01221

EndoWAM: A Grounded World-Action Model for Generalizable Endoscopic Navigation

Jinsong Lin, Zikang Pan, Wanhao Liu, Chi Kit Ng, Liangjing Shao, Zihang Yu, Ziyu Wang, Yin Wang, Jiaxi Wang, Jeremy Yuen-Chun Teoh, Zhiyong Xiong, Huxin Gao, Hongliang Ren

A video world model predicts where task-relevant target regions will appear in future frames, and a lightweight action expert shares that predictive representation to choose navigation actions in a single denoising pass. Trained on EndoMotion, a robotic endoscopy dataset spanning ureteroscopy, oesophagoscopy and ERCP, it generalises zero-shot to unseen viewpoints, environments and targets.
