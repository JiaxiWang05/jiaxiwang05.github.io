EndoWAM: A Grounded World-Action Model for Generalizable Endoscopic Navigation

Jinsong Lin, Zikang Pan, Wanhao Liu, Chi Kit Ng, Liangjing Shao, Zihang Yu, Ziyu Wang, Yin Wang, Jiaxi Wang, Jeremy Yuen-Chun Teoh, Zhiyong Xiong, Huxin Gao, Hongliang Ren

Learned endoscopy policies usually act on the current frame alone, which makes them fragile under tissue deformation, occlusion and fast viewpoint change. EndoWAM is the first world-action model for generalisable robotic endoscopic navigation. It introduces future grounding: a lightweight diffusion transformer predicts task-relevant target regions in future frames from the intermediate denoising features of a video world model, and a discrete action expert shares that predictive representation, so control runs in real time from a single denoising pass. The paper also introduces EndoMotion, a robotic endoscopy dataset spanning ureteroscopy, oesophagoscopy and ERCP. EndoWAM outperforms all baselines and alternative grounding strategies and generalises zero-shot to unseen viewpoints, environments and targets.

Cross-embodiment learning and vision–language–action systems

Robot policies are usually trained for a single platform. This project studies what lets a policy or a learned representation carry over from one robot to another.

  • How policies and learned representations generalise across different robotic platforms.
  • How vision–language-conditioned information can be integrated with action and state information.
  • Transferable action representations and multimodal robot policies.
  • Predictive representations for control, building on the world-action modelling in EndoWAM.

Keywords: embodied AI, VLA, cross-embodiment learning, action representations, world models

Small data, large models: transfer learning for medical image classification

Clinical datasets are small. This project measures which pre-trained image encoder gives the most useful retinal representations when labelled data are scarce, and whether an eye-specific foundation model is worth it compared with general-purpose ones.

Every encoder is frozen and used only as a feature extractor; the experiments are simple classifiers trained on the extracted features.

Encoders
RETFound, a retinal foundation model, against general-purpose and biomedical encoders: DINOv2, DINOv3, BiomedCLIP, ImageNet ResNet-50 and SwinV2-tiny.
Glaucoma detection on PAPILA
Patient-level splits, since PAPILA includes both eyes of each patient, with PAPILA's clinical measurements as a non-image baseline.
Data-efficiency curve
25, 50, 100 and 200 training images, ten random draws at each size, accuracy with error bars for every encoder.
External validation
Classifiers trained on PAPILA are applied without retraining to Chákṣu, captured on three cameras in a different population, and the drop is reported per camera.
If time allows
AIROGS-light to extend the curve to a few thousand images, and saliency maps to check that the models attend to the optic disc rather than artefacts.

Planned outputs: a model comparison table, the data-efficiency figure and an external-validation table.

BiliVLA: scene-aware VLA for autonomous ERCP navigation

A vision–language–action system for navigating the bile duct during ERCP, where the scene deforms and the target is easy to lose.

Results in ERCP phantom experiments

  • Supported a two-stage pipeline: grounding-enhanced supervised fine-tuning followed by GRPO reinforcement optimisation, with scene-aware grounding and safety-aware recovery.
  • Integrated the hardware, multimodal trajectories and 3-DoF actions used for closed-loop control in phantom experiments.

Trileg-ESD: vision-guided magnetic soft robot

A miniature three-legged magnetic soft robot that assists tissue manipulation during endoscopic submucosal dissection, with a cooperative VLA algorithm for two soft robots.

  • Structured controllable motion around primitives: squatting, leg lifting, rotation, crawling and stance recovery.
  • Investigated a vision–language controller that maps current and recent endoscopic frames plus a motion instruction to voltage commands for three electromagnetic coils.
